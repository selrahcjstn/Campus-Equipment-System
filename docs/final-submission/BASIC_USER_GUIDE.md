# Basic User guide

## Sign in and browse

Open the site and choose Sign In, or create a Basic User account through registration. Registration does not offer an Admin role. If an account has been deactivated, contact the campus administrator.

Open Equipment Catalog. Combine Search, Category, and Availability to find equipment; Reset filters clears the selection. Each card shows the available physical units and total serviceable units. Serviceable includes Available, Reserved and Issued, while Maintenance and Retired are excluded. The Request equipment action is available only when stock is currently available.

## Submit a borrowing request

1. Choose Request equipment on the desired catalog card. That item is already selected in Step 1. Use Add another equipment to browse additional items from any category.
2. Select one to three distinct equipment items. Use Remove to change selections. Unavailable items cannot be added.
3. Continue to Borrowing Details. Enter the quantity for each selected item, purpose and due date. The configured defaults are three units per item and seven calendar days; the service enforces the current policy and rechecks available stock when submitting.
4. Use Back to Equipment Selection if needed; entered details remain while navigating between steps. Submit Borrowing Request once and review the resulting request detail.

Pending requests do not reserve units. Availability can change before an administrator approves a request. Validation errors are displayed with the form; correct the indicated values and submit again.

## Track, cancel and return

My Borrowing Requests shows your records and decisions. Search or filter by status, and open a record for its purpose, dates, equipment, decision and reason. New request leads back to the catalog.

Pending and Approved requests can be cancelled through the permitted cancellation action. Cancelling an Approved request releases its reserved units. Issued equipment must be returned through the campus counter rather than cancelled.

My Loans shows outstanding equipment, due dates and loan status. Open a loan to see the summary and per-unit return history. Borrowing History contains your historical records. Overdue days use Asia/Manila calendar dates and apply only while units remain outstanding. The application does not calculate fines or payments.

## Account and keyboard use

Use My Profile for the permitted personal fields and password-change action. Logout opens a confirmation dialog. Tab and Shift+Tab move through controls; Enter activates links/buttons and Escape closes a dismissible modal. In narrow layouts, open the navigation menu first. Wide tables scroll horizontally rather than splitting dates and status labels.
